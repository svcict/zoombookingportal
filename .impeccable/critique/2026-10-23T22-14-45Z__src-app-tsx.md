### Priority Issues
1. [P1] Picker silently changes the minute and jumps on AM/PM (10:00 AM → PM gives 6:30 PM; then hour 02 gives 2:30 PM). Fix: keep the user's chosen hour/minute and snap only what's invalid; remember the last picked minute; consider a single 8 AM–7 PM hour axis; highlight adjusted values. Commands: harden, clarify.
2. [P1] Focus lost between steps: APPLY and "Change time" leave focus on <body>; no arrow keys in calendar/columns; read-only Name/Email are tab stops. Fix: focus the intake heading on open and the picker field on Back, roving tabindex with arrows, read-only identity as plain text. Command: harden.
3. [P2] Intake reads as a wall: 11 equally weighted settings with long help; "Zoom Verified" badge and "Agenda" pill. Fix (settings stay visible): quieter grouped sub-card, one-clause help, remove badge/pill, bordered inputs. Commands: distill, quieter.
4. [P2] Confirmation: three blue primaries; "Hosted by Sarah Jenkins"; cancelled state still shows passcode/dial-in and doesn't say whether invitees were told. Fix: "Booked by you · Zoom Account A", one blue action per state, quieter Edit/Cancel row. Commands: clarify, layout.
5. [P2] Mixed validation and unexplained rules: browser tooltip on Meeting Host; noon disabled without a reason; empty directory search gives no hint. Fix: noValidate, "12:00 noon is reserved", "No directory match. Type their full email and press Add." Command: harden.
